    How to play
    
      
    
    
    
    
      
    Find the four groups of four words that share something.
    
      
      
        
    • Select four tiles, then hit Submit to check them.
      • 
        
    • Get all four groups before you make four mistakes.
      • 
        
    • If you have three of a group right, you'll be told you're one away.
      • 
        
    • Every puzzle has exactly one solution. Words are chosen to look like they fit more than one group.
      • 
      
    
      
    Groups are colour-coded by how tricky they are:
    
      
      
        
    •  Straightforward
      • 
        
      •

    •  Sneaky

    Keyboard: arrow keys move, Space selects, Enter submits.
